About Me

I’m Mubarak, an 18-year-old software developer from Nigeria with
a passion for building modern and useful digital experiences.

I’m currently studying Computer Science at LAUTECH, where I’m
developing my foundation in technology and software
development. My journey into tech has been driven by curiosity and
a desire to turn ideas into real, functional products.

My main focus is frontend development. I enjoy creating clean,
responsive and interactive websites using technologies such as
HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Tailwind CSS. I’m particularly interested
in creating interfaces that not only look good but also provide a
smooth and enjoyable experience for users.

Beyond coding, I’m constantly exploring different areas of
technology, improving my skills through personal projects, and
challenging myself to build things that solve real problems. I believe
the best way to grow as a developer is to keep learning,
experimenting and actually building.

Mubarak Builds is a reflection of that journey — a space where I
showcase what I create, what I’m learning, and how I continue to
grow as a software developer.
